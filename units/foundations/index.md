# Foundations

Every protocol in this guide assumes a handful of small skills: running a gel, knowing how much DNA is in a tube, cleaning up a reaction, and doing the arithmetic that turns a recipe into volumes you can actually pipette. None of them belong to any single topic, which is why they get their own unit instead of being tucked into cloning or cell culture.

## What this unit will cover

I am still writing this unit, so for now this is a map of where it is headed. Each of these will get its own page:

- **Gel electrophoresis**: separating DNA by size and reading what you see.
- **DNA quantification**: measuring how much DNA you have.
- **Cleanup**: purifying DNA after a PCR, a digest, or a gel.
- **Solution math**: concentrations, dilutions, and converting a recipe into volumes.

If there is a skill you wish someone had explained to you in your first week, I would love to hear about it. Open an [issue](https://github.com/samholtzen/labmanual/issues) and we can add it to the list.
