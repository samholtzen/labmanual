# Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA)

## Introduction

DNA is an incredible biomolecule. I could wax poetic around its self-similarity, its information redundancy, and its elegant biochemical interactions all day. But I won't! Instead, I'll outline the chemical and biological relevance of the molecule, and how bioengineers take advantage of its unique properties.

## DNA, biochemically

At its most basic level, DNA is a polymer. A DNA macromolecule is composed of monomers called residues, nucleotides, or bases. These monomers are adenosine (A), guanosine (G), cytidine (C), and thymidine (T).
