# Glossary

## A

### Adherent

Cells that grow attached to a plate. See its antonym **suspension**.

### Amino acids

A chemical compound containing an amino group and a carboxylic acid group bound to the same carbon atom (the alpha carbon). They serve as the building blocks of proteins, and are also used as intermediates for other branches of metabolism.

### Annealing

The process by which base pairs undergo hydrogen bonding with their reverse complement. In the context of PCR, the annealing step happens after denaturation and allows primers to bind to their recognition sequence.
